**Jōō** (承応), alternatively romanized as **Jō-ō** or **Shōō**, was a [Japanese era name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_era_name "Japanese era name") (年号, __nengō__, "year name") after _[Keian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keian "Keian")_ and before _[Meireki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meireki "Meireki")_.
