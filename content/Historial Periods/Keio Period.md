**Keiō** (慶応, historically 慶應) was a [Japanese era name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_era_name "Japanese era name") (年号, __nengō__, literally "year name") after _[Genji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genji_\(era\) "Genji (era)")_ and before _[Meiji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meiji_era "Meiji era")_. The period spanned the years from May 1865 to October 1868.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kei%C5%8D#cite_note-FOOTNOTENussbaum2005[httpsbooksgooglecombooksidp2QnPijAEmECpgPA505_505]"Keiō"-1) The reigning emperors were [Kōmei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_K%C5%8Dmei "Emperor Kōmei")-_tennō_ (孝明天皇) and [Meiji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Meiji_of_Japan "Emperor Meiji of Japan")-_tennō_ (明治天皇).
