_**Kanbun**_ (寛文) was a [Japanese era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_era_name "Japanese era name") (年号, __nengō__, "year name") after _[Manji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manji_\(era\) "Manji (era)")_ and before _[Enpō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enp%C5%8D "Enpō")._ This period spanned the years from April 1661 to September 1673.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanbun_\(era\)#cite_note-1) The reigning emperors were [Go-Sai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Go-Sai "Emperor Go-Sai")_-tennō_ (後西天皇) and [Reigen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Reigen "Emperor Reigen")_-tennō_ (霊元天皇).[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanbun_\(era\)#cite_note-2)
