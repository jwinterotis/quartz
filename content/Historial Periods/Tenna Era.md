_**Tenna**_ (天和) was a [Japanese era name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_era_name "Japanese era name") (年号, __nengō__, "year name") after _[Enpō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enp%C5%8D "Enpō")_ and before _[Jōkyō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C5%8Dky%C5%8D "Jōkyō")._ This period spanned the years from September 1681 through February 1684.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenna#cite_note-1) The reigning emperor was [Reigen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Reigen_of_Japan "Emperor Reigen of Japan")_-tennō_ (霊元天皇).[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenna#cite_note-2)
