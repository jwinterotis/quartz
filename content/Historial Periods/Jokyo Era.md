**Jōkyō** (貞享) was a [Japanese era name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_era_name "Japanese era name") (年号, __nengō__, "year name") after _[Tenna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenna "Tenna")_ and before _[Genroku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genroku "Genroku")._ This period spanned the years from February 1684 through September 1688.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C5%8Dky%C5%8D#cite_note-1) The reigning emperors were [Reigen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Reigen_of_Japan "Emperor Reigen of Japan")_-tennō_ (霊元天皇) and [Higashiyama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Higashiyama_of_Japan "Emperor Higashiyama of Japan")_-tennō_ (東山天皇).[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C5%8Dky%C5%8D#cite_note-t415-2)
