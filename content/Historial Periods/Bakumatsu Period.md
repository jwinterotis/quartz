_**Bakumatsu**_ (幕末, 'End of the _[bakufu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakufu "Bakufu")_') were the final years of the [[Edo Period]]when the [Tokugawa shogunate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokugawa_shogunate "Tokugawa shogunate") [ended](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meiji_Restoration "Meiji Restoration"). Between 1853 and 1867, under foreign diplomatic and military pressure, Japan ended its [isolationist foreign policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isolationist_foreign_policy "Isolationist foreign policy") known as _[sakoku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakoku "Sakoku")_ and changed from a [feudal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feudalism "Feudalism") [Tokugawa shogunate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokugawa_shogunate "Tokugawa shogunate") to the [modern empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_of_Japan "Empire of Japan") of the [Meiji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meiji_era "Meiji era") [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Meiji_Japan "Government of Meiji Japan"). The major ideological-political divide during this period was between the pro-imperial [nationalists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationalism "Nationalism") called _[ishin shishi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shishi_\(organization\) "Shishi (organization)")_ and the [shogunate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shogunate "Shogunate") forces, which included the elite _[shinsengumi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinsengumi "Shinsengumi")_ swordsmen.
