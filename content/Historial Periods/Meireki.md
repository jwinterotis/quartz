**Meireki** (明暦) was a [Japanese era name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_era_name) (年号, __nengō__, "year name") of the [Edo period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo_period "Edo period"), after the [[Joo Period]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C5%8D%C5%8D_\(Edo_period\) "Jōō (Edo period)")_ era and before _[Manji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manji_\(era\) "Manji (era)")_ era. This era's period spanned the years from April 1655 to July 1658.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meireki#cite_note-1)
