**Manji** (万治) was a [Japanese era name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_era_name "Japanese era name") (年号, __nengō__, "year name") after _[Meireki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meireki "Meireki")_ and before _[Kanbun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanbun_\(era\) "Kanbun (era)")._ This period spanned the years from July 1658 through April 1661.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manji_\(era\)#cite_note-1) The reigning emperor was [Go-Sai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Go-Sai_of_Japan "Emperor Go-Sai of Japan")_-tennō_ (後西天皇).[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manji_\(era\)#cite_note-t413-2)
