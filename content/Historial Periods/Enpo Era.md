**Enpō** (延宝) (contemporarily written as 延寳) is the [Japanese era name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_era_name) (年号, __nengō__, "year name") after _[Kanbun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanbun_\(era\) "Kanbun (era)")_ and before _[Tenna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenna "Tenna")._ This period spanned the years from September 1673 to September 1681.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enp%C5%8D#cite_note-1) The reigning emperor was [Reigen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Reigen_of_Japan "Emperor Reigen of Japan")_-tennō_ (霊元天皇).[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enp%C5%8D#cite_note-2)
