# Edo Period Commercial Design (1603-1867)

## Research Question
How did commercial design practices in Edo period Japan (1603-1867) challenge or redefine Western notions of graphic design's origins?

## Timeline Overview
- [[Edo Period]] begins 1603
- [[Genroku Era]] (1688-1704) - cultural flourishing
- [[Kyōhō Era]] (1716-1736) - commercial expansion
- [[Tenmei-Bunka-Bunsei Era]] (1781-1829) - artistic innovation
- [[Bakumatsu Period]] (1853-1868) - Western contact increases
- [[Meiji Era]] 1868 - end of Edo period
