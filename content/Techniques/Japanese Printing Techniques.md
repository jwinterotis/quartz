# Japanese Printing Techniques

## Traditional Woodblock
- Early monochrome printing techniques
- Development of production workflows

## Color Printing Innovations
- [[Nishiki-e]] Full-Color Printing (1765)
- [[Suzuki Harunobu]] - Polychrome printing (1765)
  - Revolutionized ukiyo-e printmaking
  - Paved way for artists like Kiyonaga, Utamaro, Sharaku
- [[Shiba Kōkan]] - Etching techniques (1783)
  - Introduced Western-style perspective
  - Created copper plate etchings including World Map
