# Hokusai Manga (北斎漫画)

## Overview
The Hokusai Manga is a collection of sketches by [[Katsushika Hokusai]], published as 15 volumes from 1814 until after his death. The term "manga" in this context refers to "random" or "whimsical" drawings rather than the modern usage meaning comics.

## Publication History
- First volume published in 1814
- Volumes continued to be published until after Hokusai's death
- Published by [[Eirakuya Tōshirō]] in Nagoya
- Continued to be reprinted throughout the 19th century
- Became widely available throughout Japan

## Content and Structure
- **Encyclopedic Scope**: Over 4,000 illustrations covering virtually every subject
- **Categories**:
  - People and daily life
  - Flora and fauna
  - Supernatural creatures
  - Landscapes and architecture
  - Technical processes and crafts
- **Organization**: Loosely grouped by theme rather than strictly categorized

## Commercial and Design Significance
- **Design Reference**: Served as practical visual reference for artists, craftspeople, and designers
- **Technical Instruction**: Demonstrated drawing techniques and approaches
- **Visual Language**: Established standardized ways of depicting common subjects
- **Pattern Resource**: Provided motifs and designs adaptable to various media

## Technical Aspects
- **Production Method**: Woodblock printed in monochrome with light gray-blue tone
- **Visual Analysis**: Broke down complex forms into simplified components
- **Drawing Method**: Emphasized line and essential form
- **Compositional Study**: Offered multiple variations on similar subjects

## Influence and Legacy
- Used by numerous Japanese designers and craftspeople as reference material
- Served as design manual for commercial artists
- Later discovered by Western artists and designers in the late 19th century
- Influenced development of European modernist approaches to design
- Considered an early precursor to instructional design materials

## Western Comparisons
- Predated similar Western design reference works by decades
- More comprehensive than contemporary European design resources
- Achieved wider distribution than Western guild-restricted knowledge
- Combined practical application with artistic exploration

## Relevance to Research Question
The Hokusai Manga represents one of the earliest and most comprehensive design reference publications in global history. Its commercial distribution, practical application to design problems, and systematic visual approach all demonstrate the advanced state of design education and knowledge transfer in Edo period Japan. This challenges Western narratives of design systematization as primarily a 19th-20th century European development.
