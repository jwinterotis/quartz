_Shin-Bijutsukai_ (新美術海)… a Japanese design magazine that was edited by illustrator and designer Korin Furuya (1875–1910),”. These images come from a collection of issues from 1901 to 1902, bound together in a huge 353-page design book (view it online at the [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/Shinbijutsukai1) or the [Public Domain Review](https://publicdomainreview.org/collection/shin-bijutsukai-japanese-design-magazine-1902)). We can see in the traditional images of flowers and birds the influence of industrial design as well as “hints of [[Art Nouveau]] and other influences of the time” from European graphic arts.
